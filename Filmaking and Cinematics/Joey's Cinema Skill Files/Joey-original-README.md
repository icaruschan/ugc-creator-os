\## What are character locks?



A character lock is one image that does nothing but the face. Flat gray seamless background, black tank, chest-up, neutral light, dead expression, nothing in frame that isn't bone structure, skin, and identity markers. It's boring on purpose. The image model has no memory of your character between gens, every render is a fresh guess, and the lock is what you hand it so the guess lands in the same place twice. Strip the context and the face is all it can copy. Leave a scene in there and it copies that too, which is how you end up with four people who are almost the same person.

\# Voice Locking



Building character sheets, while the most fun, can also be the easiest. The hardest part is locking a style and tone of voice with that character. And we need to create some consistency. That’s where the \*\*`story-bible-builder`\*\* skill comes into play. It interviews you, pulls the world out of your head ie premise, factions, timeline, characters, their cadence, their stillness register, what they do with their hands, their hard “never say this” rules, and compiles it into a single canon doc you install as a skill.



After that it’s automatic. Every prompt you write from then on already knows who these people are. Dialogue, video prompts, image prompts, the canon comes along for free instead of you re-explaining it every session or burning memory slots trying to keep it alive.



Stop being the only backup copy of the world you’re building.
