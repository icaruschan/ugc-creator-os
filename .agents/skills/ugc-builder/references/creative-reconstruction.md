# Creative Reconstruction Reference

Use this after the reference video has been deconstructed and the user is ready to discuss a rebuild direction.

Canonical source: `workflows/node-prompts/ugc-director-v1.2.md`.

## Reconstruction Decisions

Determine or ask about:

- Content type: Organic UGC, UGC Ad, or Creative Ad.
- Format: talking head, car rant, GRWM, product demo, storytime, educational, hot take, multi-shot ad, etc.
- Platform: TikTok, Instagram, Shorts, or both.
- Goal: audience growth, sales, testimonial, education, entertainment, or creative proof.
- Preservation target: exact structure, loose vibe, motion only, hook only, product handling, voice style, or editing rhythm.

## Story Engine

Build around:

- Emotional contrast: before state -> after state.
- Hero beat: the one replayable/memorable moment.
- Sound shift: hook, hero beat, ending.
- Ending strategy: closure, loop-back, unresolved curiosity, part-two feeling, direct CTA.
- Stability risks: identity drift, hand errors, product readability, too many actions, overloaded background, weak motion.

## Conversational Checkpoint

Ask focused questions only where the user's intent changes the output:

- Keep the same script or write a new script?
- Use the same creator archetype or a new creator?
- Keep the setting or change it?
- Keep the product behavior or adapt it to a new product?
- Match voice style or create a new voice profile?
- What specific creator archetype or persona to feature?

## Rebuild Strategy Output

Keep it concise:

- What we copy from the reference.
- What we replace.
- What assets are needed.
- What can break in generation.
- What prompt system should be used next.
