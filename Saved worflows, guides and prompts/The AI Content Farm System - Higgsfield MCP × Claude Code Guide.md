## 📊 The Virality Predictor Cheat Code

Most creators skip this step. Don't.

The Virality Predictor scores every output on a 0-100 scale before you publish, based on hook strength, retention curve, scroll-stop factor, and audience pattern matching against trending content.

How to use it right:

- Always generate 3-5x more variations than you need: 50 for a 10-post week, 100 for a 20-post month.
- Score everything in one batch:

```text
Score the entire cohort with Virality Predictor.
```

- Filter top 20% only:

```text
Keep the top 20 scoring 80+ and discard the rest.
```

- For paid Meta Ads, only ship scoring 85+. This saves CAC by 30-50%.
- For organic posting, 70+ is the threshold. Volume matters more.

> **Pro move:** Run the same brief twice with different presets, score both batches, keep the winners from each. The Predictor reveals which preset works best for your niche. Bank that learning for the next campaign.
