AI Animation prompt structure
Here's my master structure for video animation:

SHOT:

•
Shot type: [SHOT SIZE]

•
Framing: [FRAMING RANGE]

•
Camera style: [HANDHELD / FIXED / STABILIZED]

SCENE:

•
Environment: [LOCATION / CONTEXT]

•
Scene motion source: [NONE / VEHICLE / CROWD / ENVIRONMENT]

CAMERA MOTION:

•
Camera attention: Locked on subject throughout

•
Motion profile:

•
Primary: [NONE / SLOW / CONTINUOUS]

•
Secondary: Subtle organic motion (micro sway, gentle vibration)

•
Shake behavior:

•
Natural, low amplitude

•
No abrupt movement

•
Consistent with scene physics

SUBJECT POSITIONING:

•
Posture: [SEATED / STANDING / GROUNDED]

•
Body movement: Minimal and restrained

•
Stability: Subject remains visually centered and readable

DIALOGUE (LIP SYNC CORE):

•
Spoken line: “[DIALOGUE TEXT]”

•
Delivery style: Calm, clear, natural

•
Lip sync requirements:

•
Accurate mouth shapes

•
Natural jaw movement

•
Subtle facial muscle engagement

•
Speech aligned to real-time motion

•
No drifting, sliding, or delayed lip movement

GESTURE BEHAVIOR:

•
Hand movement: Minimal, natural

•
Gesture type:

•
Small finger motion

•
Light wrist movement

•
Timing: Subtly synchronized with speech emphasis

•
Constraints:

•
No exaggerated arm motion

•
No large body movement

FACIAL PERFORMANCE:

•
Eye focus: Toward camera or naturally forward

•
Expression: Neutral to understated

•
Performance tone: Realistic, conversational

BACKGROUND BEHAVIOR:

•
Secondary subjects: Present but non-dominant

•
Motion: Subtle, scene-consistent

•
Focus: Soft / out of focus

•
Distraction level: Low

LIGHTING & REALISM:

•
Lighting: Even, realistic

•
Skin detail:

•
Visible pores

•
Natural texture and sheen

•
Authentic shadow falloff

•
No stylized lighting shifts

OVERALL FEEL:

•
Style: Candid, observational

•
Motion continuity: Continuous, uninterrupted

•
Realism priority: High

•
Lip sync accuracy: Critical

This is an exapmle of what it looks like in a fluid form:

A handheld medium close-up inside a moving subway car, camera focused steadily on a woman wearing a yellow top and short denim jeans seated on the bench, framed from mid-thigh to head. The camera remains trained on her as the train moves, introducing subtle, natural handheld shake and gentle vibration consistent with rail motion—small lateral sway, micro jitter, and soft breathing drift. She looks toward the camera and speaks calmly and clearly, saying: “But this is where most people still mess it up.” Lip sync is precise and natural, with realistic mouth shapes, jaw movement, and facial muscle engagement. Her body stays mostly still and grounded in her seat, with only minimal posture shifts from the train’s motion. As she speaks, she makes restrained, natural hand gestures—small movements of the fingers and wrist, light emphasis timed to her words—without exaggerated arm motion or large body movement. Background passengers sit and stand behind her, moving subtly with the train, softly out of focus. Overhead subway lighting provides even, realistic illumination with true skin texture, visible pores, natural sheen, and authentic shadow falloff. The overall feel is candid and observational, maintaining grounded realism, understated performance, and continuous handheld authenticity throughout.

This gives the tool (Whether it's kling, veo, etc.) no room for guessing, giving the best possible results.

Pro tip: The closer your subject is to the camera, the smoother the lipsync would be. Basically the more pixels the face covers, the more the tool has to work with.
