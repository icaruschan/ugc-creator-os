## Get started: Go to [Google Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow)

1. Go to Google flow
2. Hit ‘New Project’
3. Cut your video into 10s clips
4. Upload one clip at a time
5. Paste a prompt (see below)

## Prompt 1 | **Mixed-Media Collage Template**

See the video editing [**example here**](https://www.instagram.com/p/DaP1H5UoeQZ/).

You can edit the prompt below fit your script and style.

```
Edit this uploaded video and keep my face, body proportions, skin texture, clothing, movement, timing, gestures, camera framing, original voice, and original audio exactly the same.

Important: keep me photorealistic and unchanged. Do not turn me into a cartoon. Do not turn me into a drawing. Do not turn me into an animated character.

Important: preserve the microphone as part of the main foreground subject. Keep the same black desk microphone with a green button and a stand, and its position clearly visible throughout the clip. Do not remove it. Do not hide it. Do not replace it. Do not change it into another object.

Restyle the whole clip as a bold mixed-media collage with a stop-motion feel.

Cut out me and the microphone together as the main foreground subject, with a rough paper-cutout edge and a subtle white outline.

Replace the background with layered crumpled paper textures, torn newspaper scraps, halftone dots, colored tape pieces, and bold flat paper shapes.

Use a strong color palette of red, acid yellow, black, off-white, and small blue accents.

Add energetic hand-drawn arrows, circles, scribbles, sticker-style graphic elements, rough collage shapes, and tape accents around me.

The spoken line is: "Google just killed CapCut. This entire video was edited 100% by AI. Google's new video model, Google Omni, did every cut and every motion graphic you're seeing in this video right now. Comment 'Edit' on this video and I'll send it over."

TEXT POP-UPS — STRICT. Read carefully.
Add EXACTLY these three text pop-ups and nothing else, in this order, each appearing once:
1. "GOOGLE KILLED CAPCUT"
2. "100% AI"
3. "GOOGLE OMNI"

These three are the ONLY text allowed to appear anywhere in the entire video.
Do not transcribe my speech. Do not create captions or subtitles. Do not add any other words, labels, phrases, or pop-ups. Do not add "no manual editing", "every motion graphic", "edited by AI", or any variation of them. If in doubt, show fewer pop-ups, never more.

Make the three pop-ups large, bold, high-contrast, and easy to read on a phone screen.

Use slightly choppy stop-motion energy, subtle camera shake, and light glitch texture, but keep my face and the microphone clear and readable. Do not create full captions. Do not add any other words. Do not cover my face. Do not hide the microphone.

Keep the result bold, playful, fast, and visually striking.
```

## **Prompt 2 | VOX-Style Documentary Explainer**

See the video editing [**example here**](https://www.instagram.com/p/DafVJoLIswA/).

You can edit this prompt fit your script and style.

```jsx
Recreate this filmed talking-head video as a tactile, editorial explainer in a premium documentary motion-graphics style as a VOX explainer.

Important: keep my face and body exactly as filmed. Do not alter me, stylize me, or animate me. Remove echo from the audio.

Do not add subtitles or word-for-word captions. Only use occasional short title cards, labels, visual callouts, maps, article clippings, search pages, diagrams, screenshots, archival-style inserts, and data visuals when they genuinely help explain the topic.

Base every graphic, map, headline, chart, article clipping, icon, and insert on the source material from the link I provide, so all visuals make sense for the topic.

Within the first second, reframe the shot so I sit lower in the frame, leaving clear space above me. Introduce the main title or key visual in that upper space as a clean editorial motion graphic, so the frame feels like it opens up into an explainer layout.

Visual style: tactile editorial documentary. Warm cream paper backgrounds, subtle grid lines, torn and roughened edges, halftone newspaper texture, gentle film grain, light leaks, slight chromatic aberration, soft vignette, paper-cutout collage, maps, screenshots, archival inserts, and smooth camera moves across layered source material. Add small hand-drawn underlines and circled highlights as accents.

Motion style: frequent hard cuts, quick inserts, paper and card slide-ins, masked reveals, zooms into screenshots, map moves, article highlights, arrows and callouts, parallax between layers, and occasional slightly choppy, posterized animation that feels hand-made. Introduce a visual change every 1.5 to 2 seconds: a cut, zoom, insert, overlay, camera move, texture shift, map, article, or diagram. Never let the talking-head shot sit unchanged for long.

Keep the result polished, journalistic, intelligent, and editorial. Screens should feel like screens, paper should feel like paper, and every graphic should feel slightly analog rather than perfectly digital.
```
