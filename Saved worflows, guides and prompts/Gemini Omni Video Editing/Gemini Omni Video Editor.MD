## WHAT YOU NEED

- **Claude**
- **Gemini Omni Flash**
- A reference video whose style you love
- Your script (the words you want in the video)

---

## **STEP 1: FIND YOUR REFERENCE VIDEO**

Go to Instagram Reels or YouTube.

Search: `kinetic typography reel` or `motion design short` or `AI video edit style`

Find 1–2 videos with the energy you want.

Screen-record or save the file.

---

## STEP 2: OPEN CLAUDE AND UPLOAD YOUR REFERENCE

Open Claude. Upload your reference video (or screenshots from it). Paste your script too.

Then paste this prompt:

```jsx
I'm attaching a reference video and my script. I want to recreate this video's STYLE for my own script using Gemini Omni.

Step 1: Analyze the reference video. Describe:
- Animation style (2D, 3D, kinetic typography, flat design)
- How things move (speed, easing, bounce, snappy or smooth)
- Transitions (cuts, wipes, morphs, zooms)
- Color palette and background
- Typography style and where text appears
- Camera behavior (static, zoom, pan, shake)
- Overall mood and pacing
- Sound feel (whooshes, bass hits, music style)

Step 2: Break my script into scenes of 8–10 seconds each.

Step 3: For EACH scene, write one prompt that includes:
- What appears on screen and how it animates
- Exact text shown on screen (max 5–6 words)
- Colors, background, and typography matching the reference
- Camera movement and transition into the next scene
- Sound effects and music mood

Here is my script:
[PASTE YOUR SCRIPT HERE]
```

---

## STEP 3: COPY YOUR SCENE PROMPTS

Claude will give you one prompt per scene. Each scene is 8–10 seconds, the max Gemini Omni generates per clip.

You will get something like:

`Scene 1 — Hook (0–8 sec): Bold text slams in. Bass hit. Dark background.
Scene 2 — Proof (8–17 sec): Glitch transition. Zoom punch on key word.
Scene 3 — Breakdown (17–25 sec): Sticker icons pop in. Paper slap sound.
Scene 4 — CTA (25–35 sec): Spin FX. Text bounces. Crowd cheer.`

Copy each prompt. You will paste them one at a time into Gemini.

---

## STEP 4: GENERATE IN GEMINI OMNI

Open Gemini. Select the video model, **Gemini Omni Flash**.

Paste Scene 1 prompt. Attach your reference video. Hit generate.

Repeat for every scene.

> ⚠️ **Always attach your talking head video to every generation.**
> 

---

## STEP 5: FIX ANYTHING IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

If a scene looks wrong, just tell Gemini in chat. It will redo it.

`Make the text bigger
Slow down the animation
Change the background to red
Add more glitch on the transition`

Keep the best version of each clip.

---

## STEP 6: ASSEMBLE IN CAPCUT

Drop all clips into CapCut. Arrange in order. Add music or voiceover. Export.

> 📌 If any text looks messy or wrong, fix it directly in CapCut. AI still struggles with clean text sometimes. Always check before posting.
>
