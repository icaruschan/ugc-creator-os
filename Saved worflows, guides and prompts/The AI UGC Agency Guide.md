## 🧠 The 5 UGC Hook Patterns That Convert

Every high-converting UGC ad uses one of these 5 hook patterns. Train your agent on all of them. And run all 5 on every campaign.

| Pattern | Opening Line | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comparison | "I tried [product] for 30 days vs [competitor]…" | Authority + curiosity loop |
| Transformation | "I went from [pain] to [outcome] in 7 days" | Aspirational, before/after framing |
| Authority Leak | "Industry insiders won't tell you this…" | Conspiracy + exclusivity |
| FOMO | "Everyone's using this except you" | Social proof + urgency |
| Honest Review | "I bought this so you don't have to" | Reverse-psychology trust |

> **Tip:** The winner is rarely the pattern you predicted. Always test all 5.

## 🎭 How to Cast the Right AI Actor

Casting is the most underrated skill in UGC. The same script delivered by the wrong actor flops.

- **Match audience demographics first.** Age, ethnicity, vibe must match your actual buyer, not the brand's aspirational buyer.
- **Vibe over looks.** Energy and delivery beat conventional beauty every time.
- **Voice tone > face.** UGC viewers listen with their ears half the time: autoplay muted, captions later, audio re-engages on stop-scroll.
- **Test 5 actors per script.** Kill the bottom 3, scale the top 2.
- **Avoid model-perfect actors for "real person" angles.** Too polished breaks trust.
- **Match accent to market.** US English for US, UK accent for UK, etc. Mismatched accents tank CTR.

> **Tip:** Save your top 10 Arcads actors as a "favorites roster". Reuse winners across campaigns. Cast familiarity = brand familiarity.

## ✍️ UGC Script Anatomy: 5-Beat Formula

Every UGC ad that converts follows the same 5-beat structure. Write to it.

1. **Hook (0-3s):** Pattern interrupt, big claim, scroll-stop.
2. **Problem (3-7s):** The pain the viewer recognizes in themselves.
3. **Solution (7-15s):** The product, framed as the unlock, not the feature.
4. **Proof (15-25s):** Specific result, screenshot, before/after, number.
5. **CTA (25-30s):** Single action, single keyword, urgency stacked.

> **Tip:** Cut every word that doesn't drive the beat forward. UGC ads die from filler. Density wins attention.

## 📈 Creative Volume = PMF Speed

The biggest unlock isn't ad quality. It's ad volume.

- **10 ads/day beats 1 perfect ad/week.** Testing finds winners faster than craft does.
- **80/20 rule.** 20% of variations drive 80% of conversions; you can't predict which 20%.
- **Kill losers fast.** 48-hour test window, kill under 1.5x ROAS, scale above 3x.
- **Iterate hooks weekly.** Audiences fatigue at ~100K impressions per hook.
- **The agent handles volume; you handle judgment.** Read winning ads, learn what your audience wants, feed the agent better briefs next round.

> **Tip:** A solo operator running this stack ships more creative volume than a 20-person agency. The real leverage isn't speed. It's compound learning per dollar spent.

## ⚠️ 3 Mistakes That Kill UGC Ads

1. **Over-polished delivery.** UGC must feel real, not produced. Kill anything that looks like a TV ad.
2. **One hook, ten variations of the same hook.** That's 1 ad in disguise. Run the 5 hook patterns instead.
3. **Skipping the proof beat.** Claims without specifics get scrolled. Every ad needs a number, a screenshot, or a before/after.
