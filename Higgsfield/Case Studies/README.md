## 🎬 End-to-End Production Workflow

`
[Screenplay / Script]
        │
        ▼ (Use shotlist-builder.skill)
[Production Shotlist & Schedule]
        │
        ▼ (Use LIRA SKILL.md)
[Asset Generation]
   ├── Characters: 3-Panel Reference Sheets (Soul Cinema / AI Cast)
   ├── Wardrobes: Ghost-Mannequin Outfits (Seedream Pro 5)
   ├── Locations: Master Empty Plates (Soul Cinema)
   └── Composite Cleanup (Nano Banana Pro)
        │
        ▼ (Use higgsfield-seedance-prompt-builder / ACTING SKILL / CINEDANCE)
[Shot Prompts Generation] (Optics + Spatial Blocking + Action Beats)
        │
        ▼
[Higgsfield Cinema Studio / Seedance 2.0 & 2.5 4K]
        │
        ▼
[Multi-Shot Final Cinematic Video]
`

### Golden Rules for 100% Production Consistency:
1. **The Single-Face Rule:** In 3-panel character reference sheets, keep the close-up portrait face intact and erase/ghost the full-body faces to prevent Seedance from hallucinating which face to lock onto.
2. **Neutral Grey Seamless:** Always generate asset sheets and ghost-mannequin garments against a flat #7F7F7F mid-grey background with shadowless lighting.
3. **Optics Header Binding:** Begin video prompts with fixed camera specifications (e.g., *Arri Alexa Mini LF, 35mm anamorphic, 2.39:1, Kodak Vision3 500T*) so cuts across multiple scenes maintain identical grain and depth of field.
