## Before You Start

Everything in this commercial is built in two layers: first the assets, then the scenes. The assets — the car, the characters, the locations, the props — recur in every shot, so locking them first is what keeps the whole film consistent. The stills and edits were made with Soul Cinema, Seedream Pro 5 and Nano Banana Pro; every video was generated with Seedance 2.0.

You won't be writing the Seedance prompts by hand. The Claude Skill below writes them for you — you describe the scene in plain words, attach your assets, and it returns a ready-to-run prompt.
