Two things matter here. First, the grey background: after tons of testing, character sheets on grey perform way better than on white or black. Second, make sure you have one image where the face is clearly visible and one full-body for the outfit - that way Seedance doesn't have to guess anything.

Then click "Create Element," name it, and save. Do this for every character, location, and prop you make — Claude will automatically reference these images in the prompts.
